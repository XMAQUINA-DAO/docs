Robotics Capital Markets (RCM) is an onchain financial infrastructure protocol developed by XMAQUINA to bring private robotics investments onchain.

RCM provides eligible investors with access to selected private robotics investments through tokenized financial instruments designed for onchain transferability and compatibility with decentralized financial infrastructure.

## RCM Series and Assets

Each RCM investment opportunity is structured as a separate [Series](https://docs.xmaquina.io/protocol-architecture) associated with a defined underlying investment.

[**RCM Assets**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/understanding-rcm-assets) are the onchain representations of Notes issued by Humanoid Holdings Ltd. Each Note provides contractual economic exposure to the underlying investment associated with its Series.

The underlying investment is held by the Issuer, directly or through the applicable investment structure. Holders of RCM Assets have economic rights under the applicable Note terms rather than direct ownership of shares in the underlying robotics company.

## Core Functions

- Private Investment Access. Eligible investors can participate in selected private robotics investments through structured [**primary offerings**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/primary-offerings).
- Onchain Asset Issuance. Private investment exposure is represented through RCM Assets, with issuance and transfers recorded onchain.
- Permissionless Transferability. Once transferable, RCM Assets can be held in compatible wallets, transferred onchain and traded on independent [**secondary markets**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/nav-and-market-pricing).
- Investment Lifecycle Management. RCM supports subscriptions, investment acquisition, asset issuance and [**distributions following the realization of underlying investments**.](https://docs.xmaquina.io/realization-distributions)
- Asset Transparency. Investors can access information about underlying investments, independent ownership attestations, offering terms and available [**valuation disclosures**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/nav-market-pricing).

## RCM and XMAQUINA

RCM forms part of the broader XMAQUINA ecosystem, extending its focus on private robotics investments through onchain financial infrastructure.

[Protocol revenue](https://docs.xmaquina.io/fees-protocol-economics) supports ongoing operations and development, as well as initiatives directed through **XMAQUINA DAO governance**.

Participation in primary offerings is subject to [**investor eligibility**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/investor-eligibility) and applicable offering terms. RCM Assets involve investment risk, including potential loss of capital. Secondary markets operate independently of the Issuer, and liquidity, investment returns and distributions are not guaranteed.
