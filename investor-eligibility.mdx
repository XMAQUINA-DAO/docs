---
title: "Investor Eligibility"
---

Participation in RCM primary offerings is subject to investor eligibility and verification requirements, based on the investor's jurisdiction, classification and the terms of the applicable offering.

## Who Can Participate?

RCM primary offerings are primarily intended for professional, qualified and institutional investors.

Eligible investors may include:

- Venture capital, private equity and investment funds.
- Family offices and private investment companies.
- Corporate and institutional investors.
- Individuals who meet the applicable investor qualification requirements.

Eligibility is determined in accordance with applicable laws and the requirements of each Series.

## Investor Verification

Before participating in a primary offering, investors must complete the applicable eligibility and verification process through the [RCM platform](https://rcm.xmaquina.io/).

### Individual Investors

Individual investors are required to complete Know Your Customer (KYC) verification, which may include:

- Identity verification.
- Proof of residence.
- Investor classification and supporting documentation.
- Compliance and sanctions screening.

### Entity Investors

Companies, funds and other legal entities are required to complete Know Your Business (KYB) verification, which may include:

- Corporate registration and constitutional documents.
- Verification of authorized representatives.
- Ownership and beneficial ownership information.
- Supporting documentation confirming investor eligibility.

Additional information may be required depending on the investor's jurisdiction, classification and ownership structure.

Completion of KYC or KYB does not automatically establish eligibility. Investors must also satisfy the applicable investor qualification and offering requirements before being approved to participate.

## Geographic Restrictions

RCM primary offerings are available only in jurisdictions where participation is permitted under applicable laws and the relevant offering terms.

Offerings are not available to U.S. persons, persons located in the United States, sanctioned persons or investors in other restricted jurisdictions.

Availability may vary between Series and may change as additional jurisdictions become supported.

## Secondary-Market Participation

Once RCM Assets become transferable, they can be acquired, held and transferred through independent [**secondary markets**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/secondary-markets) without prior onboarding through the RCM platform.

RCM Assets are not available to U.S. persons, persons located in the United States, sanctioned persons or other persons prohibited from acquiring or holding them under applicable laws and the relevant Series terms.

Secondary-market participants are responsible for ensuring that they meet the applicable eligibility requirements.

Holders may be required to complete verification before receiving distributions or exercising certain rights associated with their RCM Assets.

## Participating in an Offering

Investors who meet the applicable eligibility requirements and receive approval can participate in available [**primary offerings**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/primary-offerings) through the RCM platform.

For more information on the investment lifecycle, see [**How RCM Works**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/how-rcm-works).
