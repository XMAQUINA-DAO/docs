title: "Treasury Attestations"
---

XMAQUINA publishes independent third-party investment verification letters from [Andersen LLP](https://global.andersen.com/) and [Aurum Solutions Ltd](https://aurum.law/) as part of the DAO’s treasury transparency framework.

The letters document governance-approved capital deployment and the Foundation’s corresponding investment interests, including direct shareholdings, interests in investment vehicles, and SAFEs.

Verification is based on executed investment agreements, payment records, and supporting transaction documentation. Each letter identifies its scope and the documents reviewed.

Attestation letters verify:

- The governance proposal authorizing the allocation
- The investment structure, legal entity, and security held
- The underlying company exposure, where applicable
- Capital committed and total capital funded, including applicable fees
- Execution and payment dates, where documented
- Supporting documentation reviewed

### **Ongoing Standard**

Treasury attestations form part of the DAO’s ongoing transparency framework and will be issued for each DAO investment once ownership has been formally secured.

Attestation letters are available above and on the individual company pages within the [DAO Portal.](https://dao.xmaquina.io/)
