---
title: "Protocol Architecture"
---

RCM connects private robotics investments with blockchain infrastructure, creating a structured foundation for investment access, onchain asset issuance and potential secondary market participation.

Through this architecture, eligible investors can acquire contractual economic exposure to selected private robotics investments in a form that supports onchain representation and transferability.

Each RCM Asset is associated with a defined underlying investment position, establishing a clear relationship between the financial instrument held by investors and the economic performance of that investment.

## **Core Architecture**

The RCM architecture brings together the following components.

- **Underlying Investment Position.** Each RCM Asset is associated with a defined private robotics investment position. The underlying investment provides the economic foundation for the asset and its potential financial outcomes.
- **Issuer.** Humanoid Holdings Ltd issues the financial instruments through which investors acquire contractual economic exposure to the underlying investment positions.
- **RCM Interest Notes.** These instruments establish contractual economic rights associated with each underlying investment position, including entitlement to an applicable share of Attributable Net Proceeds, if any, following realisation.
- **RCM Assets.** RCM Assets represent the corresponding financial instruments onchain, supporting issuance, transfers and potential interaction with compatible secondary market infrastructure.
- **Limited Recourse Pools.** Each investment arrangement has an associated Limited Recourse Pool, allowing the assets, proceeds and economic outcomes attributable to different underlying investments to be accounted for separately.

## **Connecting Private Investments to Onchain Markets**

RCM combines two complementary layers to connect private investment structures with blockchain infrastructure.

The **legal layer** establishes the financial instruments, economic rights and contractual arrangements associated with each underlying investment position.

The **onchain layer** operates on Base and uses smart contracts to support investor eligibility controls, USDC subscriptions, allocation, RCM Asset issuance and settlement. It also provides the technical foundation for asset transfers and potential interaction with compatible secondary market infrastructure.

Together, these layers provide the foundation for RCM Assets to move beyond primary issuance and potentially interact with independently operated secondary markets.

Where such markets develop, RCM Assets may support price discovery and transfers of economic exposure while the underlying robotics investment remains privately held.

Market availability and participation remain subject to applicable legal requirements and independent market activity.

## **Investment Separation**

Each underlying investment arrangement is established as a separate Series under the RCM Note Programme, with its own associated Limited Recourse Pool and records.

This structure allows different robotics investment positions to be represented through distinct RCM Assets, each with its own underlying economic exposure.

The financial outcome of each arrangement is determined by its associated underlying position and the applicable contractual terms.

## **Governance and Administration**

XMAQUINA develops the RCM Protocol and its supporting blockchain infrastructure, while Humanoid Holdings Ltd acts as the Issuer of the financial instruments under the RCM Note Programme.

Smart contracts support onchain issuance and transfers, while the Issuer maintains the Securities Ledger and administers the associated contractual rights.

The architecture combines the transferability and interoperability of blockchain infrastructure with the legal framework governing private market financial instruments.

The rights and obligations associated with each RCM Asset are defined by the applicable offering documents.
