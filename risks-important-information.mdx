## Investment Risk

Investments through RCM Protocol involve a high degree of risk, including the potential loss of the entire amount invested.

RCM Assets provide economic exposure to private robotics investments, which may be illiquid, difficult to value and subject to restrictions on transfer and realization. There is no guarantee of investment returns, distributions or secondary market liquidity.

Prospective investors should carefully review the applicable Offering Documentation and assess their ability to bear the associated risks before making an investment decision.
