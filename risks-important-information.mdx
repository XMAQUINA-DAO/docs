Investments through RCM Protocol involve significant financial, legal, operational and technological risks. Prospective investors should carefully consider these risks and review the applicable Offering Documentation before acquiring RCM Assets.

## Investment Risk

**An investment in RCM Assets involves a high degree of risk, including the potential loss of the entire amount invested.**

RCM Assets provide economic exposure to private robotics investments, which may be illiquid, difficult to value and subject to restrictions on transfer and realization.

RCM Assets do not bear interest or provide a right to investor initiated redemption. There is no guarantee of investment returns, distributions, secondary market liquidity or the timing of any realization event.

Investors should be prepared to hold their RCM Assets for an indefinite period and bear the potential loss of their entire investment.

Additional risks include those associated with the Issuer, underlying investment structures, regulatory developments, blockchain infrastructure and digital asset custody.

These considerations are not exhaustive. Prospective investors should review the full [Risk Factors](https://rcm.xmaquina.io/legal/risk-factors) available through the RCM platform.

## Nature of RCM Assets

RCM Assets are unsecured, contractually subordinated and limited recourse debt obligations of Humanoid Holdings Ltd.

They do not represent direct ownership of the underlying robotics companies or confer shareholder, voting or governance rights in those companies.

The rights of RCM Asset holders are contractual rights against the Issuer, with payment obligations limited to the assets and proceeds allocated to the relevant Series, in accordance with the applicable offering terms.

For further information, see [Understanding RCM Assets](https://docs.xmaquina.io/understanding-rcm-assets).

## Investor Eligibility & Suitability

Participation in RCM offerings is restricted to investors who satisfy the applicable legal, jurisdictional and eligibility requirements.

Satisfaction of these requirements does not establish that an investment is suitable for a particular investor.

Prospective investors are responsible for assessing the suitability of an investment in light of their own financial circumstances, investment objectives and ability to bear the associated risks.

For further information, see [Investor Eligibility](https://docs.xmaquina.io/investor-eligibility).
