---
title: "Primary Offerings"
---

Primary offerings provide eligible investors with access to new RCM Series at their initial offering terms, before the corresponding RCM Assets become transferable onchain and available for trading on independent secondary markets.

Each offering relates to a specific underlying investment and is conducted by Humanoid Holdings Ltd., the Issuer of the RCM Notes.

## Offering Terms

Each primary offering has its own investment terms, which are available through the [**RCM platform**](https://rcm.xmaquina.io/) and the applicable offering documentation.

These include:

- Underlying investment and acquisition structure.
- Indicative entry valuation for investors.
- Offering size and subscription period.
- Minimum subscription amount.
- Issue price per Note.
- Applicable fees and costs.
- Investment acquisition and issuance arrangements.

Investors should review the applicable offering documentation before participating.

## Subscription Process

### 1. Select an Offering

Explore available Series on the RCM platform and review the investment details, financial terms and supporting documentation.

### 2. Complete Investor Verification

Complete the applicable identity and compliance verification requirements.

Individual investors complete KYC verification, while companies and other legal entities complete KYB verification.

For more information, see [**Investor Eligibility**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/investor-eligibility).

### 3. Submit Subscription Funds

Investors submit their contribution in USDC through the RCM platform using their designated wallet and the network specified for the offering.

Subscription funds are deposited into the Series' onchain subscription escrow.

### 4. Sign the Subscription Agreement

Following confirmation of their contribution, investors receive the Subscription Agreement for electronic signature.

The agreement identifies the relevant Series and the investor's designated wallet and establishes the terms governing their subscription.

Investors complete the agreement during the offering period.

### 5. Eligibility Review

The Issuer reviews the signed Subscription Agreement, investor verification results and applicable eligibility requirements.

Once approved, the investor becomes eligible for participation in the offering, subject to final subscription acceptance and allocation.

### 6. Subscription Confirmation

Following the close of the offering, final allocations are determined in accordance with the applicable Series terms.

The Issuer countersigns the Subscription Agreements of accepted investors, formally confirming their subscriptions.

## Fees and Allocation

A primary offering fee of 15% is deducted from the accepted subscription amount. The remaining 85% represents the net subscription consideration used to calculate the investor's allocation of RCM Notes.

For example, for an offering with an issue price of \$1 per Note:

| Subscription details | Amount |
| :-- | :-- |
| Accepted subscription | 10,000 USDC |
| Primary offering fee (15%) | 1,500 USDC |
| Net subscription consideration | 8,500 USDC |
| RCM Asset allocation | 8,500 Notes |

Final allocations are determined in accordance with the applicable Series terms, including any allocation adjustments required in the event of oversubscription.

For more information, see [**Fees & Protocol Economics**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/fees-and-protocol-economics).

## Investment Acquisition and Verification

Following a successful offering, the Issuer proceeds with the acquisition of the underlying investment associated with the Series.

The completed acquisition is independently verified through a third-party investment attestation, with supporting investment information made available through the RCM platform.

## Refunds

If a subscription is rejected, an allocation is reduced, the offering is unsuccessful or the underlying acquisition does not proceed, investors may be entitled to a refund in accordance with the applicable offering terms.

Once refunds become available, investors can claim the applicable amount through the RCM platform.

In the event of oversubscription, investors can claim any refundable excess while retaining their accepted allocation.

Subscription funds do not accrue interest while pending allocation, acquisition or refund.

## Distribution Compliance Period

Primary offerings are subject to a 40-day Distribution Compliance Period, beginning on the later of the applicable Closing Date.

Each accepted subscriber's allocation is recorded onchain against their designated wallet, representing their entitlement to claim the corresponding RCM Assets.

During this period, investors can track their subscription status and indicative transferability date through their Portfolio on the RCM platform.

The corresponding RCM Assets are not available to claim or transfer until the applicable compliance period has expired and the Issuer has opened claims.

## Claiming RCM Assets

Following the expiry of the Distribution Compliance Period, completion of the required verification checks and the opening of claims by the Issuer, eligible investors can claim their allocated RCM Assets through the RCM platform.

Upon a successful claim, the corresponding Notes are legally issued and delivered to the investor's designated wallet.

RCM Assets become transferable upon successful claim and can be held in compatible blockchain wallets, transferred permissionlessly and traded on independent secondary markets, subject to applicable legal restrictions.

For more information, see [**Understanding RCM Assets**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/understanding-rcm-assets) and [**Secondary Markets**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/secondary-markets).
