---
title: "Understanding RCM Assets"
---

An RCM Asset is a tokenized financial instrument that provides economic exposure to a defined private robotics investment.

Each RCM Asset represents an RCM Note issued by Humanoid Holdings Ltd. under the RCM Note Program. The Notes are linked to the economic performance of an underlying investment associated with a specific Series.

RCM Assets combine exposure to private robotics investments with the transferability and interoperability of blockchain-based tokens.

## Underlying Investment and Economic Backing

Each RCM Asset is associated with a specific underlying investment held by the Issuer. This may consist of shares or other investment interests in a private robotics company.

The underlying investment, together with other assets and proceeds attributable to the Series, forms its Limited Recourse Pool.

The acquisition of each underlying investment is independently verified through third-party attestations. Information about the investment, its structure, supporting attestations and applicable offering terms is available through the [RCM platform](https://rcm.xmaquina.io/).

Each Series represents a distinct investment position. The economic performance of one RCM Asset is independent of the performance of investments associated with other Series.

For more information on the relationship between the Issuer, Series and underlying investments, see [**Protocol Architecture**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/protocol-architecture).

## Noteholder Rights and Investment Returns

RCM Notes are limited-recourse debt instruments that establish contractual economic rights against the Issuer.

The financial outcome of holding an RCM Asset depends on the performance of its underlying investment and the price at which the Asset is acquired or sold.

Holders may realize value from their RCM Assets in two ways:

- **[Secondary Market Sale](https://docs.xmaquina.io/secondary-markets).** Once transferable, RCM Assets may be sold on compatible independent secondary markets at market-determined prices.
- **Investment Realization.** Following the realization of the underlying investment, available net proceeds are distributed proportionally to Noteholders in accordance with the applicable Series terms.

For example, a holder of 1% of the outstanding Notes of a Series is entitled to 1% of its Attributable Net Proceeds, if any, subject to the applicable distribution requirements.

Holding an RCM Asset does not confer direct ownership of shares in the underlying robotics company or shareholder voting rights. Noteholders' economic rights are limited to the assets and proceeds attributable to the relevant Series.

For more information on investment exits, settlement and payments to holders, see [**Realization & Distributions**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/realization-distributions).

## Onchain Ownership and Transferability

RCM Notes are constituted as ledger-based securities under Swiss law, pursuant to Articles 973d and 973e of the Swiss Code of Obligations.

This legal framework recognizes the registration and transfer of financial instruments through a blockchain-based Securities Ledger, establishing the relationship between onchain ownership and the contractual rights associated with each Note.

RCM Assets can be held in compatible blockchain wallets and, once transferable, can be transferred permissionlessly and traded on independent [**secondary markets**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/secondary-markets) without prior RCM onboarding.

Transfers remain subject to applicable legal restrictions, and holders may be required to complete verification before receiving distributions.

## Asset Valuation

The value of an RCM Asset is linked to the economic performance of its underlying investment.

RCM publishes valuation information, including Net Asset Value (NAV) per Asset, to provide a reference for understanding the value attributable to each Note.

Once an RCM Asset trades on secondary markets, its market price is determined independently and may trade above or below its published NAV.

NAV does not represent a guaranteed redemption price. Noteholders do not have a general right to redeem their Assets at NAV or require the Issuer to sell the underlying investment.

For more information on valuation methodology, market prices and premiums or discounts, see [**NAV & Market Pricing**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/nav-and-market-pricing).

## Investment Terms

Each RCM Asset is governed by the legal and offering documents applicable to its Series. These establish the underlying investment, economic rights, fees, applicable restrictions and distribution arrangements.

Investors should review the applicable offering documentation and [**Risks & Important Information**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/risks-important-information) before acquiring RCM Assets.
