title: "Secondary Markets & Interoperability"
---

RCM Assets bring economic exposure to private robotics investments onchain, enabling transferability and potential integration with the broader blockchain ecosystem.

## Secondary Markets

Once transferable, RCM Assets may be traded on independent secondary markets, including decentralized exchanges where supported markets exist.

Secondary-market transactions take place outside the RCM platform and are conducted independently of the Issuer.

Where available, the RCM platform displays publicly available market information, including market prices, trading activity, liquidity and premiums or discounts relative to NAV.

The RCM platform does not operate a secondary-market trading venue or execute secondary-market transactions.

## Web3 Interoperability

RCM Assets are issued as ERC-20 tokens, enabling potential integration with the broader Web3 and DeFi ecosystem.

The ERC-20 standard allows independent protocols and developers to build applications around RCM Assets, extending their potential utility beyond holding and trading.

Potential use cases include:

- Additional trading pairs, liquidity pools and DEX integrations.
- DeFi lending and borrowing, including the use of RCM Assets as collateral.
- Integration into onchain investment baskets, vaults and asset management strategies.
- Portfolio tracking and onchain analytics.

Third-party integrations depend on technical compatibility, applicable legal requirements and the independent decisions of the respective protocols.

## Transaction Fees

Transfers of RCM Assets may be subject to a transfer fee applied at the token contract level.

Transactions through third-party platforms may also incur exchange fees, network transaction fees and other applicable costs.

For more information, see [**Fees & Protocol Economics**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/fees-and-protocol-economics).

## Investor Eligibility

Secondary-market transactions do not require prior investor onboarding through the RCM platform. However, the acquisition, holding and transfer of RCM Assets remain subject to applicable eligibility and jurisdictional restrictions.

Holders must complete the required verification checks before exercising rights associated with their Notes, including receiving investment distributions.

Transfers may be restricted in connection with an investment realization or other circumstances specified in the applicable Offering Documentation.

For more information, see [**Investor Eligibility**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/investor-eligibility) and [**Realization & Distributions**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/realization-distributions).
