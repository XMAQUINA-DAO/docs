---
title: "Secondary Markets & Interoperability"
---

RCM Assets bring economic exposure to private robotics investments onchain, enabling transferability and potential integration with the broader blockchain ecosystem.

## Secondary Markets

Once transferable, RCM Assets may be traded on independent secondary markets, including decentralized exchanges where supported markets exist.

Secondary-market transactions take place outside the RCM platform and are conducted independently of the Issuer.

Where available, the RCM platform displays publicly available market information, including market prices, trading activity, liquidity and premiums or discounts relative to NAV.

The RCM platform does not operate a secondary-market trading venue or execute secondary-market transactions.

The availability of secondary markets and liquidity is not guaranteed.

## Web3 Interoperability

RCM Assets use established blockchain token standards, enabling potential integration with independent financial applications and infrastructure.

Potential applications include:

- Decentralized exchanges and additional trading pairs.
- Lending and borrowing protocols supporting RCM Assets as collateral.
- Portfolio management and onchain analytics applications.

Integration with third-party applications depends on their technical compatibility, regulatory requirements and independent decisions to support RCM Assets.

## Transaction Fees

Transfers of RCM Assets are subject to applicable token-level transfer fees.

Transactions through third-party platforms may also incur exchange fees, network transaction fees and other applicable costs.

For more information, see [**Fees & Protocol Economics**](https://docs.xmaquina.io/fees-and-protocol-economics).
